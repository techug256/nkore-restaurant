OUR STORY • NKORE MEANS “LET ME TASTE”

From Ankole Firewood to Kampala’s Favourite Table

Nkore started as Mama Rose’s roadside stall in Mbarara — one charcoal stove, banana leaves, and recipes passed from grandmother to mother. Today in Kampala, we still pound our groundnuts fresh, steam luwombo for 3 hours, and roast muchomo over real embers. No shortcuts. Just home.
